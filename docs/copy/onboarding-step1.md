# Welcome to human

Your AI, locally owned.

Everything you share with human stays on your device — always. Here's what that means:

- Your conversation data stays on this device. chat.db ingest is local.
- LoRA training (when enabled) runs on your hardware, never uploads weights.
- Personal-model facts live in ~/.human/, owned 0600 by you.
- Cloud calls happen ONLY when you configure a cloud provider — and then only chat content + your prompts go to that provider; never persona, never facts.

Press Enter to continue, or type `q` to quit and resume later.
